What verification proves

A seal is a statement about a record, not about the world. Held apart on purpose:

  • Existence at a signed time
  • Integrity — unchanged since sealing
  • Post-quantum inclusion in the Merkle ledger

It never proves the record is true, lawful, safe or wise.
